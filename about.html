About

A small shop of curious things

Carousel Curios is an artist-run shop for things worth keeping: rare and vintage books, original artwork, vintage clothing, antiques and the odd collectible that doesn't fit anywhere else.

Every item is chosen, photographed and described by hand. Condition notes are honest, flaws included, so you know exactly what you're getting.

Most pieces are one of a kind. When something sells, it's gone, so if an item catches your eye, don't wait too long.
