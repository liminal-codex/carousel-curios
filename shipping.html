Shipping

Shipping

Orders ship within 3 business days of payment. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your parcel is on its way.

Packing

Books are wrapped, padded and boxed so corners and spines arrive the way they left. Artwork is shipped flat or in a tube with rigid backing.

Rates

Books ship by USPS Media Mail within the United States, usually arriving in 2 to 8 business days. Other items ship by USPS Ground Advantage. The shipping cost is shown at checkout.

International

International shipping is available on request. Send a message with the item and your country and I'll quote a price.

Local pickup

Local pickup can be arranged. Ask through the contact page.
