For shops and venues

Partner with Carousel Curios

A shelf of old books or a small case of curiosities gives customers a reason to linger. Carousel Curios supplies curated, ready-to-sell collections to local spaces.

Ways to work together

  • Consignment shelf: a curated, priced and restocked shelf or case of vintage books and objects. You keep a share of every sale, with no upfront cost.
  • Antique mall or booth: a styled booth in the shop's black and white look, restocked regularly.
  • Pop-ups and markets: a table at your event, book fair or seasonal market.
  • Styled collections: books by colour, era or subject for cafés, restaurants, hotels, offices and photo shoots, to buy or rent.

What you can expect

Every piece is cleaned, checked, described and priced, with its own item number so stock and sales are easy to track. Displays are kept tidy and refreshed with new finds.
