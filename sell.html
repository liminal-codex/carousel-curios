Sell to us

Old books, antiques and vintage, bought

Carousel Curios is always looking for good old things. If you're clearing a shelf, a house or an estate, I'd be glad to take a look.

What I'm looking for

  • Books: first editions, antiquarian and pre-1950 books, illustrated and children's classics, fine bindings, signed copies, local history, and interesting collections of any size.
  • Antiques: small furniture, decorative objects, prints and maps, ephemera, curiosities and anything with a story.
  • Vintage: well-made clothing and accessories from the 1980s and earlier, textiles, and vintage home pieces.

How it works

  1. Send a message with a few photos and what you know about the pieces. For books, a photo of the spines and the title page of anything special is perfect.
  2. I'll reply with an offer, or a consignment option if that suits you better.
  3. For larger collections I can come to you, usually within a short drive.

There's no obligation, and I'm happy to tell you what something is even if you decide to keep it.
