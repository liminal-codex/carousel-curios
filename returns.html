Returns

Returns

Every item is described carefully, with any wear or flaws noted. If something arrives not as described, you can return it within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, including shipping.

How to return

  1. Contact me within 14 days of delivery with your order details and a photo if there's a problem.
  2. Pack the item as carefully as it was sent and ship it back with tracking.
  3. Your refund is issued to the original payment method as soon as the item arrives back in the same condition.

Change of mind

Change-of-mind returns are accepted within 14 days too. The item must come back in the condition it was sent, and return shipping is paid by the buyer.

Damaged in transit

If a parcel arrives damaged, please keep the packaging and send photos straight away so a claim can be made with the carrier.
